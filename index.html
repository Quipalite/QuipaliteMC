Ranked System

Climb theTiers

A fully-featured competitive ranking system with seven tiers, per-mode leaderboards, and a permanent hall of fame for retired legends.

  • 📊
    Per-Mode Leaderboards
    Separate rankings for every weapon discipline — your Sword rank doesn't define your Mace rank.
  • ⚖️
    Head-to-Head Compare
    Go head-to-head with any player across all stats with the Compare tool.
  • 🏅
    Hall of Fame
    Retired legends are enshrined permanently with their peak stats and career achievements.
  • 🔄
    Monthly Reranks
    Rankings reset and recalibrate on the 1st of each month to keep competition fresh.
