📊 Tier Guide

Tier Evaluation

Tiers are assigned based on individual performance within each category. A player's effectiveness, consistency, and demonstrated skill are the primary factors.

Reranking Policy

Players may request a rerank within 2 days of initial ranking. After that, reranks are restricted to the monthly cycle on the 1st of each month.

Peak Tier

Peak Tier reflects the highest tier achieved in a mode — a permanent benchmark of performance.

Staff Oversight

All tiers are determined by the Quipalite MC Staff Team:

  • Aftlow — Builder & Former Player
