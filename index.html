📊 Tier Guide

Tier Evaluation

Tiers are assigned based on individual performance. Effectiveness, consistency, and demonstrated skill are the primary factors.

Reranking Policy

Players may request a rerank within 2 days of initial ranking. After that, reranks occur on the 1st of each month.

Staff Oversight

All tiers are determined by the Quipalite MC Staff Team:
